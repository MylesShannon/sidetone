Sidetone 1.2.0

A red dot instead of a warning triangle, a button for microphone access, and an offer to move to Applications.

  • The menu bar icon keeps Sidetone's waveform and gets a red dot when microphone access is off or monitoring stopped on its own. A warning triangle used to replace the waveform, which hid which app it was. After a stop, the dot goes away once you open the panel.
  • When microphone access is off, the panel says so with an Open Settings button. Switch on Sidetone in the list that opens, and within two seconds Sidetone carries on, with no restart, and starts monitoring if you'd switched it on.
  • Opened from anywhere other than Applications, such as the DMG, Sidetone offers to Move to Applications, because Open Sidetone at login and updates only work from there.
  • Opening Sidetone again while it runs shows its Settings window. Before, nothing seemed to happen.
  • While Settings is open, Sidetone has a Dock icon and menus, so ⌘W closes the window and the editing keys work.
  • When macOS needs your approval before Sidetone can open at login, Settings says so under General, beside Open Login Items Settings, and notices within two seconds once you've approved it.
