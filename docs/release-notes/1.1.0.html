Sidetone 1.1.0

Tone controls, and pickers that can follow the system default.

  • Bass and treble either side of a low cut, under Settings, Audio. The low cut takes out rumble and fan noise, and you choose where it sits.
  • Presets now carry the tone along with the devices and the gain. Presets saved by 1.0.0 still load.
  • Leave a picker on System default and monitoring follows whatever macOS is using, so putting headphones on moves it to them.
  • Monitoring can start on its own when your devices are plugged in, under Settings, General.
  • The panel closes when you open Settings, and when a display or Space change would otherwise strand it in the middle of the screen.
  • Fixes a start that switched itself off and on again, and one that gave up for good when a device went missing for a moment.
