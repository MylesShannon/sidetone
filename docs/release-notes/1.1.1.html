Sidetone 1.1.1

A fraction of the processor it used to take.

  • Monitoring with the panel closed took about fourteen per cent of a processor core. It now takes about one, which is battery back and a fan that stays quiet.
  • The panel costs less to keep open as well, and the meters move as smoothly as they did.
  • When a device you chose is unplugged, the panel names the device actually in use, rather than one that nothing is coming from.
